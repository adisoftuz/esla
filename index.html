Esla: Maxfiylik siyosati

Privacy Policy · Ilova: Esla (adi.esla) · Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 4-oktabr
O'zbekchaEnglish

1. Umumiy ma'lumot

Esla (paket nomi: adi.esla) Android va iOS uchun koreys va ingliz tillaridagi so'zlar va qo'shma gaplarni telefon qulf ekranida (lock screen) ko'rsatib yodlatadigan ta'lim ilovasi. Ushbu siyosat ilova qanday ma'lumotlarni yig'ishi, ulardan qanday foydalanishi va ularni qanday himoya qilishini tushuntiradi. Ilovadan foydalanish orqali siz ushbu siyosatga rozilik bildirasiz.

2. Qanday ma'lumotlarni yig'amiz

Akkaunt ma'lumotlari

Ilovaga Google akkaunti orqali kirasiz. Bunda ismingiz, elektron pochta manzilingiz va profil rasmingiz Google Sign-In va Firebase Authentication orqali bizga uzatiladi. Parolingizni biz ko'rmaymiz va saqlamaymiz.

O'quv ma'lumotlari

  • tanlangan til, daraja va bosqichlar, test natijalari;
  • o'rganilgan so'zlar va takrorlash jarayoni (progress, streak, ball);
  • siz qo'shgan shaxsiy so'zlar (Kundalik lug'at) va ularning jadvali;
  • ilova sozlamalari (interval, tungi rejim, shrift o'lchami va boshqalar).

Texnik ma'lumotlar

Ilova ishlashini yaxshilash uchun Firebase Analytics va Firebase Crashlytics orqali qurilma modeli, operatsion tizim versiyasi, ilova versiyasi, xatolik hisobotlari va ilovadan foydalanish statistikasi yig'iladi. Push-bildirishnomalar uchun qurilmaning bildirishnoma tokeni (Firebase Cloud Messaging) saqlanadi.

3. Ma'lumotlardan qanday foydalanamiz

  • tizimga kirishingizni ta'minlash va progressingizni saqlash;
  • so'zlar va gaplarni qulf ekranida va ilova ichida ko'rsatish;
  • xatolarni topish, ilova sifatini yaxshilash;
  • zarur hollarda sizga bildirishnoma yuborish.

Biz ma'lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz va reklama maqsadida uchinchi tomonlarga bermaymiz. Ilovada reklama yo'q.

4. Kamera va rasmlar

Rasmga olib so'z qo'shish funksiyasidan foydalansangiz, ilova kameraga yoki galereyaga kirish uchun ruxsat so'raydi. Rasmdagi matn qurilmangizda aniqlanadi (Google ML Kit). Aniqlangan so'zlarning tarjimasi qurilmada yoki tarjima xizmati orqali olinishi mumkin; bunda faqat so'z matni yuboriladi, rasm yuborilmaydi. Ruxsatni istalgan vaqtda telefon sozlamalaridan o'chirib qo'yishingiz mumkin, bunda faqat shu funksiya ishlamaydi.

5. Qulf ekrani va ruxsatlar

Qulf ekranida so'zlarni ko'rsatish uchun ilova quyidagi ruxsatlarni so'rashi mumkin: bildirishnomalar, boshqa ilovalar ustida ko'rsatish (overlay), fon xizmati va batareyani tejash cheklovidan chiqarish. Bu ruxsatlar faqat so'z kartalarini ko'rsatish uchun ishlatiladi. Ilova boshqa ilovalaringizdagi ma'lumotlarni o'qimaydi va ekranni yozib olmaydi. Ruxsatlarni istalgan vaqtda telefon sozlamalaridan olib tashlashingiz mumkin.

6. Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

Ilova quyidagi Google xizmatlaridan foydalanadi: Google Sign-In, Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics) va Google ML Kit. Bu xizmatlar ma'lumotlarni o'z maxfiylik siyosatlariga muvofiq qayta ishlaydi: policies.google.com/privacy va firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7. Ma'lumotlarni saqlash va o'chirish

Ma'lumotlaringiz akkauntingiz mavjud bo'lgan davrda saqlanadi. Akkauntni va unga bog'liq barcha ma'lumotlarni ilovaning Sozlamalar bo'limidagi "Akkauntni o'chirish" tugmasi orqali o'chirishingiz mumkin. Ilovaga kira olmasangiz, quyidagi elektron pochtaga o'chirish so'rovini yuboring, biz uni 30 kun ichida bajaramiz. Qonun talab qilgan hollarda va xavfsizlik zaxira nusxalarida ayrim ma'lumotlar qisqa muddat saqlanib qolishi mumkin.

8. Xavfsizlik

Ma'lumotlar uzatilganda shifrlangan (HTTPS) aloqa orqali yuboriladi va Firebase xavfsizlik qoidalari bilan himoyalanadi: har bir foydalanuvchi faqat o'z ma'lumotlariga kira oladi. Biroq internet orqali uzatishning mutlaq xavfsizligini kafolatlab bo'lmaydi.

9. Bolalar

Ilova 13 yoshgacha bo'lgan bolalarga mo'ljallanmagan va ularning shaxsiy ma'lumotlarini bila turib yig'maydi. Agar bola ma'lumot taqdim etganini aniqlasangiz, biz bilan bog'laning, ma'lumotni o'chiramiz.

10. Sizning huquqlaringiz

Siz o'z ma'lumotlaringizga kirish, ularni tuzatish, nusxasini olish va o'chirishni so'rash huquqiga egasiz. Buning uchun ilova sozlamalaridan foydalaning yoki quyidagi manzilga yozing.

11. O'zgarishlar

Ushbu siyosat vaqti-vaqti bilan yangilanishi mumkin. Yangi versiya shu sahifada e'lon qilinadi va yuqoridagi sana o'zgaradi.

12. Aloqa

Savollar yoki ma'lumotni o'chirish so'rovi uchun: adisoftuz@gmail.com

Privacy Policy (English)

App: Esla (package: adi.esla) · Effective date: October 4, 2026

1. About this policy

Esla is an education app for Android and iOS that helps you memorize Korean and English words and compound sentences by showing them on your phone's lock screen. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Data we collect

Account data

You sign in with your Google account. Your name, email address and profile photo are provided to us through Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your Google password.

Learning data

  • selected language, level, stages and test results;
  • learned words and review progress (progress, streak, points);
  • personal words you add (Daily Vocabulary) and their schedule;
  • app settings (interval, night mode, font size and others).

Technical data

To keep the app working well, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics collect device model, operating system version, app version, crash reports and usage statistics. A device notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) is stored to deliver push notifications.

3. How we use data

  • to sign you in and save your progress;
  • to show words and sentences on the lock screen and inside the app;
  • to find bugs and improve the app;
  • to send you notifications when needed.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for advertising. The app contains no ads.

4. Camera and photos

If you use the "add words from a photo" feature, the app asks for camera or gallery access. Text in the picture is recognized on your device (Google ML Kit). Translations of recognized words may be obtained on the device or through a translation service; only the word text is sent, not the photo. You can revoke the permission in your phone settings at any time; only that feature will stop working.

5. Lock screen and permissions

To show cards on the lock screen the app may ask for: notifications, display over other apps (overlay), a foreground service, and exemption from battery optimization. These permissions are used only to display word cards. The app does not read data from your other apps and does not record your screen. You can remove these permissions at any time in your phone settings.

6. Third-party services

The app uses these Google services: Google Sign-In, Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics) and Google ML Kit. They process data under their own privacy policies: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data with the "Delete account" button in the app's Settings. If you cannot access the app, email a deletion request to the address below and we will complete it within 30 days. Some data may remain for a short time in security backups or where the law requires.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by Firebase security rules so each user can access only their own data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, obtain a copy of, and request deletion of your data. Use the in-app settings or write to the address below.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page and the date above will change.

12. Contact

For questions or a data deletion request: adisoftuz@gmail.com
